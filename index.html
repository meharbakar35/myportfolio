What I take off your plate

Most brands split this across a developer, a media buyer and a content person, then lose their week translating between them. I cover the whole channel, so the store, the catalogue, the ads and the creative all pull in the same direction.

  • Store development

    Custom Shopify builds and Liquid theme work, a checkout set up for how your customers actually pay (cash on delivery and online), app and payment gateway integration, speed fixes, domains and DNS, and conversion work on the pages that take the order.

  • Shopify store management

    The day to day running of the store: product and collection listings with clean titles, variants, images and SEO, inventory and price updates, discounts and sales, order and customer handling, shipping rates and courier flow, and sales reports that show what is actually selling. Daraz listings too.

  • Paid growth

    Meta, TikTok and Google Ads campaign structure, pixel and conversions API tracking, audience and creative testing, and daily calls on what to scale and what to cut. Reported in the numbers that matter: cost per order, ROAS and delivered revenue.

  • Content and creative

    UGC style video ads, product shoot direction and scripts, and ad copy written for the feed and the product page. Every creative starts from a specific hook, never made first and explained later.
